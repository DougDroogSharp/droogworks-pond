Dramaton — the narrative engine

Born 1987 inside King of Chicago. Resurrected 2026. Still telling stories.

What it is

Dramaton is a narrative engine: characters, pressures, and scenes computed into drama. It first ran the streets of King of Chicago (Cinemaware, 1986–87), giving every player a different gangster movie. Its script language, Dramscript, is being recovered page by page from the original manuscript scans.

State of the resurrection

  • A complete Dramaton game editor lives in GitHub — fairly advanced, resurrected and analyzed in 2026
  • The Dramaton Editor redesign is an active workstream, with a shared design brief across working instances
  • Original Dramscript is being extracted from King of Chicago manuscript scans

First job on the platform

Phrog's onboarding will be a story, not a form — a narrative interaction that assesses a new Mage's alligators, suggests Familiars, covers the ethics of the pond, and asks what Familiars the Mage already has. That story is Dramaton's natural commission: the 1987 engine writing the 2026 welcome.
