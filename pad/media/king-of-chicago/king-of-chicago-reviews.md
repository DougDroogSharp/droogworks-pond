# King of Chicago — the reviews
*Selected from the press archive, 1987–now. The best, the best-of-the-best, and the most lukewarm on file.*

---

## The raves

> "The King of Chicago is yet another triumph for the Cinemaware team… brilliantly devised game that far outstrips others of its genre."
>
> — **Personal Computer World**

> "King of Chicago represents a landmark in computer gaming… the game is unique, and represents a positive step toward software movies."
>
> — **The MACazine**

> "The King of Chicago is unlike any game currently available… Overall value: 10."
>
> — **ConsumerGuide**

> "The game of the month is King of Chicago on the Amiga 2000."
>
> — **Jerry Pournelle, Byte**

> "Game of the Month… richest set of characters, both in number and substance, of any Cinemaware title."
>
> — **Amazing Computing**

> "This hybrid movie/game… consistently entertaining… saturated with plot and characters from great 1930s gangster flicks."
>
> — **MacWorld**

> "King of Chicago is innovative, interesting, and a 'must-get' for gamers tired of killing aliens."
>
> — **A+ Magazine**

> "It is the attention to detail that makes this one a real pleasure… Each one has a unique personality."
>
> — **Amiga Sentry**

> "Realistically speaking, the King of Chicago stands out from the pack. Indispensable to the Mac owners."
>
> — **SOS Aventure** (France)

## The long view

> "I don't think people realize what a landmark achievement in game development it was… so far ahead of its time, it's scary."
>
> — **Casey Muratori**, game developer

> "King of Chicago is one of Cinemaware's best-loved games… one of the best gangster games ever made… Recommended!"
>
> — **Home of the Underdogs**
