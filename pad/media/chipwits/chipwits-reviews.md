# ChipWits — the reviews
*Selected from the 1985–2008 press archive. All killer, no filler — the archive contains no pans, and not for lack of looking.*

---

## The awards shelf

> **Top 10 Apple II/Mac Games of all time**
>
> — **MacLife**, March 2008

> **Editor's Choice 1985**: "…icon based language is wonderfully innovative, and the program's design is both refreshing and highly educational."
>
> — **MacUser** (5-mouse review)

> **Best of '85**: "…so much fun you won't even realize you've been educated until you leave the environment."
>
> — **MACazine**, which called it "revolutionary" and predicted it would "become a cult favorite among Macintosh users"

## The raves

> "ChipWits is a program that every Mac user should have."
>
> — **Byte** (Product of the Month)

> "If you have only one entertainment/educational program in the disk cabinet next to your Macintosh, ChipWits should be it."
>
> — **Icon Review**

> "A fantastic program that is as educational as it is entertaining… a world where playing and learning become (as they should be) one and the same."
>
> — **INFO64** (5 stars)

> "Not too many reviews come with a guarantee, but this one guarantees that if you like programming, you'll love ChipWits."
>
> — **MacWorld**

> "ChipWits is delightful and delightfully easy to use… highly educational as well as highly entertaining… very challenging and even addictive on the more difficult play levels…"
>
> — **Commodore Power/Play**

> "ChipWits is an excellent game, both in concept and implementation."
>
> — **COMPUTE!'s Gazette**

## The review that says it all

> "Last night my wife came downstairs at 4:30 a.m. only to witness me cursing at a cartoon robot."
>
> — **Creative Computing**, December 1985

*The closest thing to a complaint on record — and it's a five-star complaint.*
